Screening gates

A trial must pass each gate, in this order, to appear.

  1. Condition: the patient's tumour, or a broader family that includes it, is a listed condition.
  2. Status: the trial is recruiting, not yet recruiting, by invitation, or available as expanded access.
  3. Age: the patient is inside the registered age range and any age limit stated in the summary.
  4. Sex: the patient fits any sex restriction.
  5. Biomarker: EGFR, IDH and MGMT status do not conflict with what the trial selects for.
  6. Disease setting: newly diagnosed and recurrent trials only take matching patients.
  7. Geography: the trial has a site in the patient's country, or lists no sites yet.

Score

Passing trials are ranked out of 100: condition 35, geography 25, biomarker 20, status 10, disease setting 5, study design 5. Records not updated for over two years lose 5 points. Strong is 75 or more, Good is 55 to 74, Possible is below 55.

Limits

  • Biomarker and disease-setting rules read free text, so they can miss or misread a requirement.
  • Distance is grouped by city, state and country, not measured.
  • Performance status, prior treatment, tumour volume and organ function are not assessed.
  • The rules have not been validated against clinician decisions.
